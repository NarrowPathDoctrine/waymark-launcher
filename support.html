Need help?

If you experience a problem with Waymark Launcher, use the support option provided inside the app or the developer contact option shown on the Waymark Launcher Google Play listing.

When reporting an issue, it helps to include your Android version, Waymark Launcher version, device model and a short description of what happened.

Permissions and Android settings

Some features depend on Android permissions or special access, including notifications, calendar information and local weather. If a feature stops working, review Waymark's permissions in Android settings and confirm that the related access has not been disabled.

Privacy

For information about data handling and permissions, read the Waymark Launcher Privacy Policy.
