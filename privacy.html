Waymark Launcher

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Overview

Waymark Launcher provides a customizable Android home screen and optional tools for weather, web search, reminders and shortcuts. Waymark does not require an account. Your launcher preferences and user-created information are primarily stored on your device. Waymark does not sell personal information or use your launcher data for targeted advertising.

Information processed on your device

Depending on the features you choose to use, Waymark may access or store the following information to provide the launcher:

  • Installed app information, your selected Home apps and folders, customized app names and hidden-app choices.
  • Theme and appearance settings, imported wallpaper images, fonts and other launcher preferences.
  • Quick reminders you create, including their text, completion status and display preferences.
  • Quick actions you configure, including selected system actions, app shortcuts, custom labels and the contact names or phone numbers associated with call or text shortcuts you specifically choose.
  • Web search history saved within Waymark, together with your search-provider and suggestion preferences.
  • Location information and cached weather results used for local forecasts.
  • Calendar information used to show upcoming events when calendar access is enabled.
  • Notification information used to display and interact with notifications when Android Notification access is granted.
  • Media-session information, such as track title, artwork and playback status, when media controls are available.

Waymark's contact-based Quick actions use Android's contact picker. You choose the individual contact and number for a call or text shortcut. Waymark does not browse, collect or back up your entire contacts list.

Waymark Now and Quick reminders

Waymark Now offers optional web search, a local forecast, Quick reminders and Quick actions. You can enable or disable the page and its individual sections in Waymark Now settings.

Quick reminders and their completion state are saved locally in Waymark's preferences. Reminder text is not sent by Waymark to a reminder service or a server operated by Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries.

Quick actions are locally stored selections for Android settings, installed apps, app shortcuts and contact-based call or text actions. Opening an app, website, dialer or messaging app may cause that destination to process information under its own privacy practices. Waymark does not independently place calls or send text messages through these shortcuts.

Web search and websites

Waymark supports Google and DuckDuckGo for web search. When you submit a search, your search terms are sent to the provider you select.

  • Search engine suggestions: Partial search text is sent to the selected search provider while you type to obtain live suggestions.
  • On-device suggestions: Suggestions use local information and saved search history without sending partial search text to a search provider while you type. Submitting the search still contacts your selected provider.
  • Suggestions Off: Autocomplete and recent-search suggestions are not displayed as you type.

You can open web links using Waymark's built-in viewer or a browser. Websites and search providers may receive information normally associated with web requests, such as your IP address, browser information and information you choose to submit. They operate under their own privacy policies.

Location and weather

When you use local weather, Waymark may obtain your approximate location through Android or use a location you select. The coordinates used for a forecast request are sent to the external weather provider, Open-Meteo, to retrieve conditions and forecast information. Weather information may be cached on your device for display and refresh purposes. Waymark does not use your location for advertising.

Backups, restore and reset

When you choose Backup Waymark, Waymark creates a portable ZIP backup containing launcher preferences and imported wallpaper and font files. It includes Home organization, themes, Waymark Now settings, saved Quick reminders and their completion states, Quick action assignments, and locally saved search history.

May Contain Sensitive Information: Your backup may include reminder text, recent web searches, personal wallpaper images, and phone numbers you have chosen to assign to Quick actions. It does not include your entire phone contacts list.

Waymark backup files are not encrypted. Store them securely and avoid sharing them with anyone you do not trust. You choose where to save the backup. If you select cloud storage or another external destination, that provider may store or process the file under its own privacy policy. Waymark does not automatically upload your backup to a server operated by Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries.

Restoring a Waymark backup replaces the current Waymark setup with the saved preferences and assets. Some third-party app shortcuts may need to be selected again if the related apps or shortcuts are unavailable.

Reset Waymark Launcher clears locally saved launcher preferences, imported theme assets, Quick reminders and customized Quick actions. Clearing Waymark's app data through Android also removes its local data. Backup files you saved outside Waymark's app data remain wherever you placed them until you delete them.

Permissions and device access

Waymark uses Android permissions and system-provided access for features you choose to enable, such as approximate location for weather, calendar access for upcoming events and Notification access for notification and media features. Selecting a wallpaper, font or contact uses Android's relevant picker.

You can review and revoke permissions in Android settings. Disabling access may limit the corresponding feature without preventing use of unrelated launcher features.

Data storage and retention

Launcher settings, reminders, Quick actions and other locally saved information remain on your device until you edit or clear them, reset the launcher, clear app data or uninstall Waymark. Waymark does not maintain a separate user account database for this information. User-created backup files are managed at the storage location you choose.

Advertising and sale of data

Waymark does not sell personal information or use calendar events, reminders, selected contacts, search history, notifications, media sessions or launcher preferences for targeted advertising.

Google Play, Android and other services

Waymark runs on Android and is distributed through Google Play. Google, your device manufacturer, selected search and weather providers, websites, apps opened through Quick actions, and storage services you choose may process information under their own privacy policies. Waymark does not control those services' separate data practices.

Children's privacy

Waymark is not designed as a service for collecting personal information from children. It does not require a Waymark account or maintain a user-profile database.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised to reflect Waymark's features or data practices. The date at the top indicates the latest policy revision.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, use the developer contact information on Waymark Launcher's Google Play listing.
