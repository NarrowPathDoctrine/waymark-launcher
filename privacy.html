Waymark Launcher

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Overview

Waymark Launcher is designed to provide a personalized Android home screen while keeping most launcher information on your device. Waymark does not require an account and is not designed to sell personal information or share personal information for advertising.

Information processed on your device

Waymark may process information locally in order to provide launcher features. Depending on the features you enable, this can include:

  • Your installed apps so Waymark can display and launch them.
  • Home-screen organization, folders, hidden apps, theme choices, icon settings, fonts, wallpapers and other launcher preferences.
  • Recent searches or similar convenience history that you choose to keep within Waymark.
  • Calendar information used to show an upcoming event on the home screen.
  • Notification content when Android notification access is enabled, so Waymark can display, open and dismiss notification cards.
  • Media-session information such as track title, artwork and playback state when media controls are shown.

This information is used to provide the requested launcher features and is not sent to a server operated by Narrow Path Doctrine Ministries unless a specific network feature described below requires an external request.

Location and local weather

If you use local weather features, Waymark may use a location supplied by Android or a location you select in order to request weather information. The location used for a weather request may be sent to the weather service needed to return the forecast. Waymark does not use location information for advertising.

Waymark Now, search and news

Waymark Now can provide web search, search suggestions, weather information and news stories. These features may require network access depending on the options you choose.

  • When you submit a web search, the search terms are sent to the search provider you selected. Waymark currently supports Google and DuckDuckGo for web search.
  • If Search engine suggestions are enabled, partial search text is sent to your selected search provider as you type so that provider can return live autocomplete suggestions.
  • If On-device suggestions are selected, suggestions are generated from Waymark's local suggestion data and search history on your device. Partial search text is not sent to a search provider while you type.
  • If search suggestions are turned Off, Waymark does not request live autocomplete suggestions or display local autocomplete suggestions while you type.
  • News feeds, article images and story information may be requested from external news or content providers.
  • If you open a news story, search result or other website, that website may receive information normally sent with a web request, such as your IP address, browser information and any information you choose to provide to that site.

Third-party search engines, news sources, weather services and websites operate under their own privacy policies and are responsible for their own data practices.

Permissions

Waymark requests Android permissions or special access only when needed for features you choose to use. Examples may include calendar access, notification access and location access for local weather. You can review or revoke permissions through Android settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working but does not prevent use of unrelated launcher features.

Data storage and retention

Launcher settings and other locally stored information remain on your device until they are changed, cleared through Android or an applicable in-app option, or removed when the app is uninstalled. Waymark does not maintain a separate user account database for launcher settings.

Advertising and sale of data

Waymark Launcher does not sell personal information. Waymark is not designed to use the contents of your calendar, notifications, media sessions, app list or launcher configuration for targeted advertising.

Google Play, Android and other platform services

Waymark is distributed through Google Play and runs on Android. Google, Android system components, your device manufacturer and other platform services may independently process technical, diagnostic, security, store or device information under their own privacy policies. That processing is controlled by those services rather than by Waymark Launcher.

Children's privacy

Waymark does not knowingly operate a service intended to collect personal information from children. The app does not require a Waymark account or maintain a user-profile database.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Waymark's features or privacy practices change. The revision date at the top of this page will show when the policy was last updated.

Contact

For privacy questions about Waymark Launcher, please use the developer contact information provided on the Waymark Launcher Google Play listing.

This policy describes the current Waymark Launcher design. It should be reviewed whenever new analytics, advertising, account, cloud-sync or data-collection features are added.
