Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about setting up and using Waymark.

How do I make Waymark my default Home app?

Open Waymark Settings and choose Change default launcher under Launcher & system. Android will open the Home app chooser so you can select Waymark Launcher.

Why are weather or calendar information not showing?

Those features need the related Android permission before Waymark can show their information. Once calendar or weather/location access is granted from Waymark, the related feature is enabled automatically. If access was previously denied, you can review Waymark's permissions from Android App info and then enable the feature again.

What is the difference between Waymark Themes and My Themes?

Waymark Themes are ready-made presets included with the launcher. My Themes contains themes you create or customized versions you save yourself. Your saved themes remain independent from the original built-in presets.

Will applying a theme change my Lock Screen wallpaper?

Not unless you choose that behavior. Built-in Waymark themes use the Built-in theme Lock Screen option in Settings. Themes saved under My Themes remember their own Also use on Lock Screen choice from Theme Studio.

How do I back up and restore Waymark?

Use Waymark's Backup & Restore option in Settings. A Waymark backup can restore the launcher setup covered by the backup system, including themes, Home items, folders, hidden apps, Waymark Now settings and preferences.

What happens if I clear or reset Waymark's app data?

On an existing installation that has been reset, Waymark can show a Welcome Back screen with options to restore a previous backup or start fresh. Starting fresh begins normal onboarding again from the beginning.

How do Waymark Now search suggestions work?

You can choose Search engine, On-device or Off in Waymark Now settings. Search engine suggestions use live suggestions from Google or DuckDuckGo and send partial search text to the selected provider as you type. On-device suggestions stay local to Waymark, while Off disables suggestions entirely.

How do notification cards work?

If you enable Android Notification access for Waymark, the launcher can show notification cards. Tapping a notification opens its destination, and dismissing a card removes it through Android's notification system. You can turn Notification access off again from Android settings at any time.
